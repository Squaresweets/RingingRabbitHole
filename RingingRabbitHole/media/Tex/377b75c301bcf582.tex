A capital letter is a row. It can also be thought of as a permutation if you think of it as a permutation from rounds to that row. $XY$ is applying permutation $X$ to row $Y$. $I$ is rounds, also known as the identity row. Exponentiation means repeated application. $X^0=I$, $X^1=X$, $X^2=XX$, etc. $X^{-1}$ means the inverse permutation of X, such that $X^{-1}X=I$. $H$ is the first lead head of plain bob doubles. $A$ is the row we looked at in the previous section. Note that permutations do not commute (we cannot guarantee $XY=YX$).
\end{flushleft}
