For those of you that don't know, Complib is a website created by Graham John for creating and sharing methods and compositions. The general tab is where we input settings for the composition as a whole, the methods tab is where we can add methods to splice between, the calls tab lets us define custom calls and the calling tab lets us lay out our composition. This is where you will be spending the majority of your time!

Finally, in the calling tab you can see our composition. You can press validate to see the generated composition.

The way to read this table is left to right, top to bottom. Whenever you see a dash inside a cell that means that there is a bob in the calling position at the top of that column. It is also possible to force a coursehead at a certain lead by putting a colon after a call. This notation only works when using numeric calling positions.
